The problem

CVRP, the instances we run, why now.

What is CVRP?

Vehicles of capacity Q leave a depot, visit each customer once, return. Minimize Euclidean distance. NP-hard. Exact methods do not reach city scale.

Motivation

Keep what works — MST + randomized DFS (ParMDS), 2-opt refine — and engineer it for many cores: angular split, implicit edges, custom heap. Million-customer logistics is the target, not another 200-node toy.

How it solves

α-buckets from the depot

Split customers into angular cones of width α. Run MST + ρ randomized DFS on each cone, in parallel, then 2-opt and merge.

  1. 01
    Partitionk = ⌈360°/α⌉ buckets. Depot in every bucket. No complete graph.
  2. 02
    Conquer on coresNested OpenMP: buckets × ρ trials. Implicit Euclidean weights.
  3. 03
    StitchRefine each bucket, union routes. Feasible CVRP, minutes not days.
